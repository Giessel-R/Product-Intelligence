What is this?

The Product Intelligence OS is an automated competitive intelligence system built for Ping Identity. It watches competitors, understands my products, and delivers a weekly strategic briefing.

help_outline The Problem

As a Director of Product at Ping Identity, I need to understand what our competitors are doing, including which features Okta and Auth0 are shipping, whether they are building capabilities Ping does not have, and whether there are product gaps we should be paying attention to.

The problem is not access to information. The problem is the cost of processing it. Competitors publish updates continuously. To stay current, I would need to review dozens of pages every week, compare what I find against Ping’s own capability map, and translate raw observations into strategic recommendations. That is slow, repetitive, and time-consuming.

bolt The Solution

This system automates the entire pipeline:

1
Sense
Watch competitor websites, changelogs, docs, and GitHub repos for relevant changes every weekday. Market Watch visits every URL in the config list, hashes the content, and detects changes via MD5 comparison.
2
Understand
Turn raw page changes into structured evidence that can be reviewed and used for product analysis. Each change is classified by strategic lane, scored for importance and confidence, and separated into observed facts versus analyst interpretation. Claude Haiku handles the classification step, and no finding is logged unless it first passes a relevance gate.
3
Compare
Compare competitor moves against Ping Identity’s own product capability map, refreshed weekly from Ping’s product documentation. The Competitor Intelligence workflow uses Claude Sonnet to identify real gaps, areas of parity, and Ping advantages, with source-quality checks to prevent overconfidence.
4
Recommend
Produce a weekly newsletter with gap analysis, strategic signals, and recommended actions, delivered to my inbox every Friday morning, and published to Notion for later review.
adjust Current Scope

Phase 1: Okta and Auth0 only. The system is designed to expand to 10 competitors by adding rows to a Google Sheets config tab, no code changes required.

Phase 2 targets: Okta, Auth0, Transmit Security, CyberArk, Frontegg, Descope, Strivacity, IBM Verify, Microsoft, One Identity.
