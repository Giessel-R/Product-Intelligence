Weekly Schedule

The system runs automatically Mon–Fri at 6:30 AM ET. Market Watch runs every day; deeper analysis runs on a staggered schedule to match data availability.

schedule
Run Timing
All runs trigger at 6:30 AM ET, Monday through Friday via cron 30 6 * * 1-5. The Master Orchestrator handles all scheduling — sub-workflows do not have their own schedules.
calendar_today Day-by-Day Schedule
DayWorkflowsWhy
Monday Market Watch + Internal Product Specialist Week starts fresh. IPS rebuilds Ping's capability map so CI has current data for Tuesday and Thursday analysis.
Tuesday Market Watch + Competitor Intelligence First CI run of the week. Uses Monday's updated capability map. Processes any evidence collected Mon–Tue.
Wednesday Market Watch only Mid-week collection. No analysis — just accumulating evidence for Thursday's CI run.
Thursday Market Watch + Competitor Intelligence Second CI run. Processes Wed–Thu evidence. Gives two structured analysis sessions per week for the newsletter to pull from.
Friday Market Watch + Competitor Intelligence + Newsletter + Memory & Audit Full pipeline day. Collect → Analyze → Write → Audit. Newsletter is emailed by ~7 AM. Memory & Audit wraps up the week.
account_tree Data Flow Through the Week

Market Watch runs first every day, building a continuous stream of evidence in the evidence_log. On Monday, IPS refreshes Ping's capability map in internal_capability_map — this is the "know yourself" baseline that CI compares against.

On Tuesday and Thursday, Competitor Intelligence reads the last 3 days of evidence from evidence_log, compares against the capability map, and writes structured findings to structured_findings. On Friday, the Newsletter workflow reads from structured_findings and produces the weekly briefing.

Memory & Audit runs last on Friday — it scans the week's findings for items that need human review (low confidence + high relevance), and writes a quality summary to execution_log.

warning_amber
Evidence Cutoff
Competitor Intelligence reads evidence from the last 3 days only. Gaps in the schedule (e.g., a missed Wednesday run) mean that evidence from those missed days may fall outside the CI window and not be analyzed.
