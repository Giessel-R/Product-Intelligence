AI Model Tiers

The system uses three Claude models, each matched to the complexity of its task. The guiding principle: use the cheapest model that can do the job correctly.

Claude Haiku

bolt Tier 1 · Fast

The background scanning model. Haiku runs on every changed page in Market Watch — potentially dozens of calls per day. It's fast, cheap (~$0.001/call), and accurate enough for classification and relevance scoring tasks where the prompt is structured and the output schema is fixed.

Used by

Market Watch — Receives 3,000 characters of page content. Returns a structured JSON object with: summary, observed_fact, analyst_interpretation, lane, importance_score, confidence_score, evidence_strength, not_enough_evidence.

~$0.001 per call · Input cap: 3,000 chars of page content

Claude Sonnet

psychology Tier 2 · Deep Analysis

The reasoning model. Sonnet is used for tasks that require nuanced judgment: understanding Ping's own product documentation, comparing capability tiers against competitor evidence, and writing the newsletter. Sonnet is significantly more capable than Haiku for tasks involving long context, structured reasoning, and multi-step instructions.

Used by

Internal Product Specialist — Reads Ping documentation pages, extracts structured capability records into the internal_capability_map schema. Applies per-source extraction guidance and maturity vocabulary.

Competitor Intelligence — Receives tiered capability map per lane and evidence batch. Produces gap/parity/advantage analysis with maturity-aware claims and confidence scoring.

Newsletter — Writes the weekly competitive intelligence briefing in up to 600 words, structured across 6 sections. Returns formatted HTML for Gmail delivery and a Notion page body.

~$0.01–$0.05 per call · Long context inputs · 3 workflows

Claude Opus

star Tier 3 · Phase 3

Reserved for the Monthly Strategy Memo — a Phase 3 feature not yet built. Opus will synthesize a full month of competitive signals into an executive-level briefing: cross-competitor patterns, strategic themes, and recommended priorities. It runs once per month (1st Friday) and is not cost-effective for high-frequency tasks.

Planned for

Monthly Strategy Memo (Phase 3) — Executive synthesis. Cross-competitor pattern detection. Long-horizon strategic framing. One call per month.

Not yet built · Phase 3 · ~1 call/month
info
Cost Design
The system is designed to stay under $15/month by using Haiku for all high-frequency scanning and reserving Sonnet for low-frequency deep analysis. The relevance gate (Stage 4) reduces Haiku calls further by dropping low-signal pages before they reach Claude.
